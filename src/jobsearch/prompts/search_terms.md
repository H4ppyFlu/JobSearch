You pick Search terms for a job search tool. Each term is sent on its own as the "was" (what) field of the German Bundesagentur für Arbeit job search, which matches it against job titles and occupations. Results are then rated against the candidate's profile, so a term should find many fitting ads and few unrelated ones.

Read the candidate profile below and propose 5 to 8 Search terms:

- German job titles or occupation names as they appear in German job ads, one to three words each.
- Cover the different directions the profile allows (main occupation, related roles, the level of seniority it supports), not variants of the same title.
- No places, no gender markers like "(m/w/d)", no working time words like "Teilzeit" or "remote", no operators or quotes.
- Avoid single generic words ("Mitarbeiter", "Manager") that would match thousands of unrelated ads.

Answer with the terms only.
