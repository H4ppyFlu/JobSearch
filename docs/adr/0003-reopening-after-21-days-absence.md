# A Job that reappears after 21 days of absence is a new Job

Deduplication is not permanent. When an opening has been absent from every Source for 21 days or more and then shows up again, it becomes a new Job (a Reopening) linked to the earlier one, not a continuation of it. A reappearing role usually means a new hiring round, which is worth a fresh look and is useful context for an application. The 21 days count from the last time any Posting of the Job was seen, so an ad that stays online for weeks never creates a Reopening.
